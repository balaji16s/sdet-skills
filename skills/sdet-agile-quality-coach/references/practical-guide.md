# Agile Quality Coach: Practical Guide

## Conversation Checklist

Ask how a change moves from an idea to usable software. Where do developers, testers, and business representatives compare their understanding? Where do data, environments, specialist support, or reviews cause delays? Include people who operate the product when their knowledge matters.

Pick the smallest intervention that addresses an observed problem. A team missing business rules may need earlier examples, not more automated UI tests. A team waiting for credentials may need access preparation, not another meeting.

## Example

A team receives refund rules only after coding. Propose a short discussion between the product owner, developer, and tester before the next refund change. Record agreed examples and unanswered questions. Check whether late clarification and rework decrease; do not promise a particular improvement percentage.

## Boundaries

Recommend practices, but do not change tickets, team policies, or calendars without a request. Broader capability improvement belongs with the process-improvement skill.

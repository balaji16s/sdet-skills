# Example Mapping Facilitator: Practical Guide

## Simple Map Format

Story S1: user outcome and scope.

Rule R1: business rule, source, and confirmed/proposed status.
- Example E1: starting situation → action → expected result.
- Example E2: boundary or contrasting case.
- Question Q1: unresolved point, why it matters, decision owner.

Color is optional. Use labels so the map remains readable in plain text and by people who cannot distinguish colors.

## Example

Story: apply a delivery discount.
Supplied rule: orders of at least 50 currency units receive free delivery.
Examples: 49.99 does not qualify; 50 qualifies, assuming two-decimal amounts are supported.
Question: is the threshold calculated before or after other discounts?
Do not choose an answer to this question without a source. Link any affected examples to it.

## Facilitation Checks

Invite business, development, and testing perspectives where available. Ask what example would disprove the current interpretation. Avoid letting the first suggestion or the most senior voice become an unexamined rule.

Many questions suggest missing knowledge. Many unrelated rules may suggest splitting the story. Neither requires an automatic numerical threshold. Take agreed examples into acceptance criteria or test design only after review.
